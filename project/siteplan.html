Website Project Plan

This document outlines the purpose, audience, visual identity, and proposed homepage layout for the S-Man Group website developed as part of WDD 131.

1. Site Name

S-Man Group — Technology and Business Solutions

The name S-Man Group represents a South African business focused on technology and a range of business solutions. It retains the organization's established identity and communicates a professional, service-oriented brand.

Existing domain: smangroup.co.za

Brand tagline: Built on Trust, Driven by Ambition.

2. Site Purpose

The website will introduce S-Man Group, explain its services, and help businesses and organizations understand how the company may support their needs. Visitors will be able to explore service information, learn about the company, and submit an enquiry through a contact form.

Target Audience

The primary audience includes small and medium-sized businesses, organizations, and prospective clients in South Africa seeking technology and business services.

Planned Pages

  • Home: Company introduction, key services, and navigation to other pages.
  • Services: Descriptions of technology and other business solutions.
  • Contact: Contact information and a form for prospective clients to submit service enquiries.
  • References: Sources for externally sourced images and written content.

3. Visitor Scenarios

  • What technology and business solutions does S-Man Group offer?
  • How can my organization enquire about a service?
  • Where can I find information about the company and its work?

4. Color Schema

The color palette follows the existing S-Man Group identity. Navy communicates professionalism, gold provides visual emphasis, and white keeps the content readable.

Navy Blue

#001F3F

Header, footer, major headings, and navigation.

Gold

#D4AF37

Accents, decorative borders, and selected highlights.

White

#FFFFFF

Main background and text on dark backgrounds.

Body text will use a dark neutral color on white backgrounds. Text and interactive elements will be checked for adequate contrast before submission.

5. Typography

Cinzel — Headings

Cinzel will be used for the main title and section headings to reflect the established S-Man Group branding.

Roboto — Body Text

Roboto will be used for paragraphs, navigation, lists, labels, and form controls because it is clear and readable on mobile and desktop screens.
